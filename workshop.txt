description=This mod adds a trait called "A Really CDDA" (and 2 others, less hardcore traits, one that only spawns fires without injuring the player and one that only injures the player without spawning fire)
description=
description=What the trait does: (stuff differ from the original CDDA challenge)
description=
description=- Gives + 1 point.
description=(If you want this trait to give more - or zero - points, you can adjust your character starting points in the Sandbox Settings -> Character -> Free Trait Points. I made it give one point so that the trait is displayed as a negative one.)
description=- Player starts drunk, wet, and with a nasty cold.
description=- Player also starts panicked.
description=- No items, no clothes, no belt.
description=- Only a key ring in their inventory since to my knowledge it's not lootable anywhere.
description=- There's a deep wound with glass shard lodged into it on the player's groin.
description=- There's also a high chance to get a scratch on either a leg, foot or torso. (leg 40% chance, foot 20% chance, torso 10% chance, left arm 20% chance, no scratch 10% chance)
description=- The building the player spawns in is on fire.
description=- A few fires have spawned in the vicinity outside, too.
description=
description=
description=If you want to base your Custom Sandbox game on the CDDA challenge settings, here they are:
description=- Population -> Zombie Population = Insane
description=- Time -> Start Month = December
description=- World -> Water Shut Off = Instant
description=- World -> Electricity Shut Off = Instant
description=- World -> House Alarm Frequency = Rare
description=- World -> Locked House Frequency = Rare
description=- World -> Months since the Apocalypse = 12
description=- Nature -> Erosion Speed = Very Fast (20 days)
description=- Nature -> Nature's Abundance = Very Poor
description=- Loot Rarity -> All on Extremely Rare
description=- Advanced Zombie Options -> Population Multiplier = 4 (should have been automatically set by the Zombie Population setting on Insane already)
description=- All other settings = Default Value.
